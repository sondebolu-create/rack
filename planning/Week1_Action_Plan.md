## Capture system details

### Screen recording

**iPhone**
- Settings → Control Center → add Screen Recording.
- Swipe down from the top-right to start. Long-press the button to turn the mic on.

**Android**
- Quick Settings → Screen recorder. If it's missing, tap the pencil icon to add it.

**Every phone recording:** switch on Do Not Disturb first and use a clean home screen.

**Windows**
- Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar), or Snipping Tool video mode.
- OBS (free) records full sessions.

**Mac**
- Cmd + Shift + 5 to record. Screen Studio (paid, Mac only) auto-zooms on your cursor.

**Habit:** record every Claude Code session. You'll only use 10–20 seconds of each, but you can't film a moment after it's gone.

### The running note

| Section | What you write | Example | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lessons | One line a day: what surprised you, broke, or you'd change | "Oct 28: schema built fast but missed RLS on cart_items. Always ask for policies explicitly." | Day 20 (lessons), Day 15 |
| Numbers | Weekly snapshot from Nov 20 (Sundays) + build milestones | "Nov 29: TikTok 312, X 540, IG 410, waitlist 86, RackGPT tests 40, matches ≥80%: 31" | Day 10, Day 21 |
| Costs | Every spend: tool, amount, date | "Oct 27: Claude Pro $20 / Apple Developer $99 / domain $12" | Day 20 video (cost breakdown) |

**Two tips:**
- Date every entry, so the recap posts can tell the story in order.
- Log numbers even when they're small. "12 followers → 400" makes a better recap than only the final figure.
